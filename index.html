Where the water comes from
  1. River intake — water enters the pumping system.
  2. Heated pipeline — carries raw water 3 km to the plant.
  3. Treatment & storage — filtration and disinfection before trucks load.
  4. Delivery trucks — load, visit household tanks, return to the plant.

Trucks do not normally visit the river intake or travel through the pipeline. GPS cannot reveal upstream pumping or treatment time.

How timings are estimated

GPS position and time inside a stop area can suggest an activity. They cannot confirm pumping, loading, filling, treatment completion or water safety. This demo generates GPS-style positions and stop times; it has no real GPS data.

Loading: 25–35 minutes. Household stops: 6–10 minutes. Queue: 10 minutes, plus one deliberate 2-hour staging wait. These are demonstration assumptions, not measured Inukjuak averages. The countdown is a plan, not a GPS measurement.

Six illustrative shift starts are staggered two hours apart; the fleet size and schedules are not the municipality's reported operations.

KRG report: water-system sequence · APTN: household fills take a few minutes in nearby Puvirnituq. No verified local loading duration was found; treatment and pipeline durations remain unknown.
